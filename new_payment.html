Record an incoming payment

The payment is saved with status Received and status-history record 1. Then you can match or return it.

What the database checks
  • amount > 0 AND amount = ROUND(amount, 2)
  • length(senderName) BETWEEN 1 AND 100
  • FOREIGN KEY (vIbanId) → VirtualIBAN
  • status DEFAULT 'Received'

Try -50, 12.345 or an empty sender name to see SQLite reject the insert.
